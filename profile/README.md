# BondForge

Forging Innovation in Bail Bonds

## What is BondForge?

Many bail bond agencies still rely on pen and paper, aging software, or systems that simply weren't designed around the way bondsmen actually work.

BondForge is being built to change that.

Our goal is to meet bondsmen and their agencies where they are — whether that's behind a desk, at the jail, in court, or working from a phone in the middle of the night. BondForge is designed to give agencies modern tools for writing, tracking, and managing bonds from virtually any device without forcing them to completely rethink the way they do business.

Technology should adapt to the people doing the work, not the other way around.

## The Origin of BondForge

BondForge has been years in the making.

In 2010, Uncle Joey Bail Bond Management entered the market as an earlier attempt to give bonding companies a better way to track and manage their bonds. It solved real problems, but it was also a product of its time and came with the limitations of the technology available then.

BondForge takes the lessons learned from Uncle Joey and starts fresh with today's technology, today's security expectations, and a much broader vision for what bail bond software can be.

This isn't simply Uncle Joey rebuilt on a newer framework. BondForge is the next generation.

## Security Is Part of the Foundation

Bail bond agencies handle highly sensitive information every day. At the same time, the software engineering world is moving faster than ever.

We believe those two realities make deliberate engineering and information security more important, not less.

Security isn't something we intend to bolt onto BondForge before launch. It is being considered throughout the architecture, development, testing, and deployment of the platform.

That means taking the time to test our assumptions, challenge our systems, examine failure modes, and cross our T's and dot our I's before asking agencies to trust BondForge with their businesses and their clients' information.

Moving quickly matters. Earning that trust matters more.

## Release Timeline

BondForge entered pre-alpha development and testing in late Q2 2026, marking the beginning of hands-on validation of the platform beyond its initial development stages.

Alpha testing is planned to begin in Q4 2026, with a limited group of early participants helping us test BondForge under increasingly realistic conditions and provide feedback as we continue toward release.

We haven't announced an official public release date.

We're building toward one deliberately.
